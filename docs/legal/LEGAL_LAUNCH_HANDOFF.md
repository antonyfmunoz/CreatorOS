# CreativesOS legal launch handoff

CreativesOS currently publishes factual operational policies for account
deletion, community conduct, AI/recording/synthetic voice, and the Trust
Center. These pages describe implemented product controls; they are not a
substitute for binding Terms of Service or a jurisdiction-specific Privacy
Policy.

## Product evidence ready for counsel

- Clerk-backed identity with separate login and registration routes.
- Machine-readable account export with credentials and private storage
  locators removed.
- Seven-day reversible deletion workflow, ownership-transfer blockers,
  private-object erasure, shared-message redaction, pseudonymized retained
  ledgers, and retried Clerk deletion.
- Stripe platform revenue and creator-connected-account earnings are modeled
  separately.
- Community membership, moderation, reporting, and enforcement evidence.
- Recording, transcription, relationship intelligence, AI authority,
  synthetic-voice consent, disclosure, approval, and retention controls.
- Provider adapters remain separate from canonical identity, consent,
  entitlement, relationship, and audit data.

## Required operator decisions before publishing binding documents

1. Exact contracting legal entity and any doing-business-as name.
2. Registered business address and public notices address.
3. Privacy, legal, copyright, and support contact addresses.
4. Governing law, forum, arbitration/class-waiver position, and age floor.
5. Countries/states offered at launch and whether children can use the service.
6. Marketplace seller status, platform fee/refund/chargeback rules, tax role,
   creator payout timing, and prohibited goods/services.
7. Copyright/DMCA agent and repeat-infringer process.
8. Data-controller/processor roles, subprocessors, international transfers,
   retention schedule, regional privacy rights, and cookie/analytics choices.
9. AI training/data-use position and user license for content distribution.
10. Final counsel approval and displayed effective date/version.

Do not publish generated placeholders for these fields. Once the operator
decisions and legal review are complete, add versioned `/legal/terms` and
`/legal/privacy` pages and link them from both Clerk authentication flows,
checkout, seller onboarding, and the Trust Center.
